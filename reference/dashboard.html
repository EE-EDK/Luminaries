Architecture

Functional ES modules, no classes. main.js bootstraps, populates the world with a seeded RNG, then runs one animation loop whose per‑frame work is a scheduler of named systems. Cross‑cutting concerns flow through a typed event bus.

Source: architecture.md, src/kernel/, src/systems/registration.js. Seed 42.

Budget

These are hard limits. Flag alternatives before any risky change; never spend the frame rate on a feature.

Source: performance.md, fps‑hotspots, core/lighting.js.

Frame ratefloor 20 · seen 31–59
Target avg ≥ 20 and 1%‑low ≥ 15, never below 15. The observed band is from the 2026‑06‑07 playtest HUD; the formal per‑scenario table is still unmeasured.
Real‑time lights8 of 8
1 hemisphere, 2 directional (moon, moon2), 1 player, 1 orb, 3 crystal proximity pool (MAX_CRYSTAL_LIGHTS = 3). The crystal pool is the only flex. Never add ambient or directional lights.
Draw callsest. 80–120 of 200
Static estimate only. Confirm live with LumiDebug.perf() and read draws. Anything with more than 50 copies must be an InstancedMesh.
Particles per systemfireflies ~120 of 500
Eleven pooled systems, each capped below 500. Pools are pre‑allocated Float32Arrays and never grow.

Hot‑path rules

  • No allocations in the animation loop. Pre‑allocate at module scope.
  • Squared distances only in culling checks. No Math.sqrt() in the hot path.
  • InstancedMesh for anything with more than 50 copies; 500+ also needs LOD and culling.
  • Share materials. Lift non‑modulated materials to module scope with a _ prefix. Clone only when emissiveIntensity is animated per instance.
  • depthWrite: false on every transparent, glow, or haze material.
  • Keep emissiveIntensity between 0.3 and 2.0 or bloom blows out.
  • Bloom internal resolution is capped at 512² and re‑applied after resize, because setSize() ignores .resolution. Shadow map updates at ~1 Hz.

flag it before you add

  • More than 500 particles in one system, or any new real‑time light.
  • A per‑frame loop over more than 200 entities without culling.
  • A canvas texture over 1024², or a new post‑processing pass.
  • Any Math.random() or new inside the loop.

Audio

Everything is synthesized in systems/audio.js and systems/music.js. No audio files. Entity files never import audio; they emit events or receive callbacks through main.js.

Source: audio.md, procedural‑audio protocol.

Bus graph

AudioContext └─ masterGain → destination ├─ forestGain → forestFilter → brown noise (co‑prime loop lengths) ├─ forest2Gain → second brown layer ├─ windGain → bandpass 200–800 Hz → brown noise ├─ rainGain → bandpass 1200–3200 Hz → white noise ├─ waterGain → waterFilter → stream noise (pond proximity) ├─ reverbGain → dual delay 0.55 s / 0.79 s (shared send) ├─ musicMasterGain (0.6) → harp, flute, drone, chime └─ transient voices: creatures, footsteps, interactions, hum

Rules

  • First line of every public function: if (!initialized || muted) return;
  • Per‑voice volume 0.02 to 0.08. Always call .stop(time) so nodes do not accumulate.
  • Route through connectWithReverb(gain, masterGain, wet).
  • New triggers: emit a kernel event and subscribe in initAudio(). Callback injection stays for legacy paths.
  • Sanctioned exceptions, not bugs: thunder burst at 0.20 / 0.15 (short transient, exponential decay) and music voices at 0.15 pre‑bus, about 0.038 delivered after 0.6 × 0.42.

adding a sound

  1. Add a guarded function in audio.js: oscillator or noise → gain envelope → connectWithReverb(), then .stop().
  2. Export it, then either subscribe to an event in initAudio() or pass it as a callback from main.js.

Quest and narrative

The forest is dimmed in five angular sectors. Each orb, once activated with the right creature's frequency, sends a restoration wave through its sector and raises the obelisk one rung. Five orbs end in a finale, a transform, and free roam.

Source: MANIFESTO.md, GAME_GUIDE.md, narrative.md, quest/config.js.

Quest phases

  1. SEEKfind and activate orbs
  2. RISINGobelisk climbs at 0.8 u/s
  3. COMPLETE12 s · glitter, moat, rainbows
  4. FINALE30 s · creatures gather
  5. TRANSFORM13 s · lasers to every tree, white flash
  6. FREE_ROAMdimming gone, all rings active

The orb sequence, from code

Two‑phase unlock

  1. HumF, sweep with Q/E or the slider; creature within 20 m

  4. Carrylight changes color; decays; jelly needs a re‑click within its window

The Dimming

  • Five angular sectors from the world center; DIMMING_FACTOR = 0.18 in unrestored sectors.
  • Activation sends a restoration wave at 30 m/s with edge blending.
  • Every restorable emissive reads getLocalGlow(x, z, bioGlow); grass does it on the GPU via uGlowMult.
  • Restored zones: fairy rings grant a 3.5× jump with 4 s feather fall; bubbles pop with a pulse. Dimmed zones: bubbles repel.

Environmental literacy

  • Constellations: one revealed per orb, 3 s fade, in world/sky.js.
  • Shooting‑star wishes: look up (pitch < −0.5) as one crosses; five levels gated by orbs.
  • Ground glyphs: stand within 3 m for about 2 s to reveal a hidden marker.
  • Crystal chains: three crystals in sequence fire a perfect‑fifth tone and a wave pulse.
  • Echo‑visions: sprint in a storm to see glowing traces of creature paths.
  • Obelisk runes: one face per orb in the creature's color; the next face is pre‑lit as a breadcrumb.

Rules and patterns

Source: CLAUDE.md, patterns.md.

Conventions

  • const and let only. No classes; functional style throughout.
  • Named imports from Three.js and from project modules, for tree‑shaking.
  • Colors come from C in constants.js. Never inline a hex value.
  • Seeded sr() for world generation; Math.random() only for runtime variation.
  • Banner comments // ===…=== for major sections. Module‑scoped state, not closures or globals.
  • DOM overlays for intro, discovery and HUD text; troika-three-text for in‑world 3D text.
  • Logic modules never import core/input.js: it constructs a WebGLRenderer and breaks Vitest. Thread inputs through parameters.
  • Register per‑frame work with addSystem(); do not grow main.js.

Adding an entity

  1. Count constant and colors in constants.js.
  2. src/entities/<category>/name.js exporting makeName().
  3. Call it in populate(), after flat zones.
  4. Register an update via addSystem() or extend a _director* function.
  5. Check the light budget if it emits light.
