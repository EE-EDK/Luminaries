## 1. Design intent

Two axes, both asked for by the owner:

- **Immersion** — the forest should feel inhabited and continuous: things happen
  whether or not the player is watching, sound has a location, weather touches
  the world instead of overlaying it, and the camera behaves like a body.
- **Gameplay value** — progress is kept, the core verb (humming) is taught by
  the world rather than by a hint string, and the game has a reason to be
  re-entered after the finale.

Constraints that shape every item below (from CLAUDE.md, non-negotiable): 20 FPS
floor, 8 real-time lights, no allocations in the loop, squared distances in hot
paths, seeded world generation, colours from `C`, DOM overlays for text. The
Phase 2 descope list still holds: no free flight, no custom shader pipeline or
chromatic aberration, no avatar mesh, no separate ending cutscenes, no Howler.

## 2. Workstreams

M ≈ a session, L > a session), **risk** to the FPS floor, and **how it is verified**.

### B. Onboarding and readability — teach the hum with the world

| # | Item | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Diegetic first-encounter beat.** The first time a creature is within 8 m and the player is still, its resonance band glows on the slider and a single narrative line names the action for *this* platform (F / slider). One-shot per creature type. Replaces reliance on the idle hints. | S | none |
| B2 | **Platform-correct hints everywhere.** The two idle hints in `discoveries.js` (~line 225) still say "Press F … Q or E" on phones. Route every control name through one helper (`controlName('hum')`) so desktop and touch never diverge again — same pattern as `isHumInputActive()`. | S | none |
| B3 | **Lock feedback.** The 2 s pitch-lock currently reads only as a thumb pulse. Add a ring closing around the crosshair (DOM) and a rising tone; the ring's colour is the creature's band. | S | none |
| B4 | **Wayfinding without a map.** Wisps and dandelion seeds already bias toward the next orb; make the *player light* lean toward it for 1 s after each restoration wave, so the world points the way at exactly the moment the player looks up. | S | none |

*Verify:* wizardHumGate-style FSM test for B1 (fires once, right string per

### C. Atmosphere and rendering

| # | Item | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Fog colour and density follow phase and weather.** `FogExp2` is a fixed colour today; tint it toward the sky's horizon colour per day phase and lift density in MISTY / FOG_BANK (weather already has `fogMult` — wire it). The far forest's `IMPOSTOR_HAZE` should read from the same value so trees and air agree. | S | none |
| C2 | **Vignette + subtle grain** in the existing saturation `ShaderPass` (one pass already runs; this is a few lines of GLSL in it — not a new pipeline). Reduced-motion turns grain off. | S | none |
| C3 | **Dawn / dusk light shafts**: 6–10 additive billboards angled from the sun through the canopy, only during DAWN/DUSK, fading with weather. Zero lights, ~8 draw calls. | M | low |
| C4 | **Wind gusts as events.** The motion shader already takes `uWindAmp / uWindLeanX / Z`; today they change slowly. Add a gust system: every 20–60 s a front crosses the world (direction + speed), driving the uniforms, a leaf burst, and a swell in the ambient bed. | M | none |
| C5 | **Rain touches things.** Ripple rings on pond surfaces (ripples exist — spawn them from rain), drip particles under canopy edges, wet-sheen boost on rocks (`roughness` dip on the shared rock material). | M | low |
| C6 | **Camera as a body.** Sprint FOV kick and landing dip exist; add look smoothing (settings), a small lean into strafes, and a breath sway when still for > 3 s that the reduced-motion setting disables. | S | none |
| C7 | **Puffling houses readability** (open TODO): emissive windows and door glow tied to `bioGlow`, plus the impostor treatment now used for trees so distant villages read. | M | low |

### E. Creature life

| # | Item | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Daily rhythm.** Deer bed down at DAWN under the nearest tree (target = nearest `trees_data` entry, one lookup per deer per phase change); pufflings gather at their houses at DUSK; moths appear only after dark; jellies rise at NIGHT and sink at DAWN. Uses the existing steering targets — no new AI. | M | none |
| E2 | **Weather reactions.** In HEAVY_RAIN / LUMINOUS_STORM, pufflings and deer seek canopy (same nearest-tree target); moths ground themselves; jellies drift lower. | S | none |
| E3 | **Creatures notice each other.** Pufflings follow a nearby deer for a while; moths orbit a resting deer's antlers; a jelly passing over a fairy ring makes the ring pulse. Three rules, each a squared-distance check in an existing update loop. | M | low |
| E4 | **Reaction to the player's light.** As the player light evolves, creatures within 6 m turn toward it for a beat before resuming — a single lerp on an existing look target. | S | none |

*Verify:* faunaSmoke-style tests that the new states never throw and that each
creature type has a target after a phase change; headless count of "bedded"
deer at DAWN.

### G. Performance hardening — the last Phase 2 item, Debt 5

| # | Item | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **GPU rig for fauna.** Deer are 17 meshes because each pivot animates on the CPU; at the origin they cost 123 draw calls, jellies 72, pufflings 40, moths 30. Extend `_motion.js` with a **rig mode**: bake a joint index per vertex, upload per-creature joint quaternions as a small uniform array (deer: 17 joints), and let the shader pose the mesh. Deer → 3 meshes (solid / glow / unlit), jellies → 2, moths → 2. The CPU gait code keeps writing the same angles; only the consumer changes. Estimate: fauna 283 → ~60 draws at the origin; total ~670 → ~450. | L | medium — must hold 20 FPS with the extra uniform uploads; measure per step |
| G2 | **Instance fauna by type** once rigged (all deer share one InstancedMesh per role, joint arrays as instanced attributes). This is the step that approaches the 200 budget: fauna ~60 → ~15. | L | medium |
| G3 | **Per-type animated-creature cap** as the cheap fallback: only the nearest N of each type animate; the rest hold a pose. | S | none |
| G4 | **Measure on real hardware.** `LumiDebug.perf()` at the four probe points on the 3090 and on one phone, before and after each of G1–G3, written into `reference/performance.md`. | S | — |

*Verify:* single-frame draw-call probe (existing script), `entityBudget.test.js`
ceilings updated, FPS table in the doc.

## 3. Sequencing — four sprints, each ends deployed

Ordered by player-visible value per unit of risk. Each sprint ends with tests,
build, the draw-call probe, a deploy, and an owner playtest on the 3090 and a
phone.

| Sprint | Theme | Items | What the player notices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Keep what you earned** | persistence + clarity | A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, B3, C1, C2, C6, I1, I2, J2, J5 | Progress survives a reload. The hum is taught by the first creature. The air has colour. |
| **2 — The forest is alive** | creature life + sound | E1, E2, E3, E4, F1, F2, F3, F4, C4, C5, C7 | Deer bed down at dawn, sound comes from where things are, rain lands on the pond, gusts move the whole canopy. |
| **3 — Reasons to stay** | notebook + free roam | D1, D2, D3, D4, I3, I4, C3, C8 | After the finale there is a book to fill, and dawn has light shafts to fill it under. |
| **4 — Runs everywhere** | performance + structure | G1, G2, G3, G4, H1–H4, J1, J3, J4 | Steadier frame rate, phones hold the quality floor, draw calls halved. |

Sprint 1 is deliberately the least risky and the most felt; Sprint 4 is the only
one that can threaten the FPS floor, so it goes last and is measured per step.

---

## 4. What this plan does not claim

- **No FPS numbers are asserted.** Every "cost" above is engineering time; the
  frame-rate effect of C3, C5, G1 and G2 is unknown until measured on hardware.
- **Audio items cannot be verified from the sandbox.** F1–F4 need ears.
- **The 200 draw-call budget is not promised.** G1+G2 is the credible path
  toward it; the current honest estimate after both is 300–450, not 200.
- **The five open TODOs are not all covered.** Puffling houses (C7) and debug
  discoverability (partly via I3 / A2) are; the jelly ritual, the receptive
  encounter flow and the 2nd-phase gate need a dedicated playtest session first
  to establish whether they are still broken after the 2026-07-01 fixes.
