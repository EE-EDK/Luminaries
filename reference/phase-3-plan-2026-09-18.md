What that says about the game today: the world is dense and finally visible to
the horizon, the core loop works end to end, but a player who closes the tab
loses everything, a new player has to discover the hum mechanic by reading a
hint line, sound comes from "left-ish / right-ish" rather than from a place, and
once the finale plays there is nothing left to do.

---

## 1. Design intent

Two axes, both asked for by the owner:

- **Immersion** — the forest should feel inhabited and continuous: things happen
  whether or not the player is watching, sound has a location, weather touches
  the world instead of overlaying it, and the camera behaves like a body.
- **Gameplay value** — progress is kept, the core verb (humming) is taught by
  the world rather than by a hint string, and the game has a reason to be
  re-entered after the finale.

Constraints that shape every item below (from CLAUDE.md, non-negotiable): 20 FPS
floor, 8 real-time lights, no allocations in the loop, squared distances in hot
paths, seeded world generation, colours from `C`, DOM overlays for text. The
Phase 2 descope list still holds: no free flight, no custom shader pipeline or
chromatic aberration, no avatar mesh, no separate ending cutscenes, no Howler.

### C. Atmosphere and rendering

### E. Creature life

### G. Performance hardening — the last Phase 2 item, Debt 5

| # | Item | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **GPU rig for fauna.** Deer are 17 meshes because each pivot animates on the CPU; at the origin they cost 123 draw calls, jellies 72, pufflings 40, moths 30. Extend `_motion.js` with a **rig mode**: bake a joint index per vertex, upload per-creature joint quaternions as a small uniform array (deer: 17 joints), and let the shader pose the mesh. Deer → 3 meshes (solid / glow / unlit), jellies → 2, moths → 2. The CPU gait code keeps writing the same angles; only the consumer changes. Estimate: fauna 283 → ~60 draws at the origin; total ~670 → ~450. | L | medium — must hold 20 FPS with the extra uniform uploads; measure per step |
| G2 | **Instance fauna by type** once rigged (all deer share one InstancedMesh per role, joint arrays as instanced attributes). This is the step that approaches the 200 budget: fauna ~60 → ~15. | L | medium |
| G3 | **Per-type animated-creature cap** as the cheap fallback: only the nearest N of each type animate; the rest hold a pose. | S | none |
| G4 | **Measure on real hardware.** `LumiDebug.perf()` at the four probe points on the 3090 and on one phone, before and after each of G1–G3, written into `reference/performance.md`. | S | — |

*Verify:* single-frame draw-call probe (existing script), `entityBudget.test.js`

### K. The two stories, and the third one underneath — narrative cohesion (added by owner, 2026-09-18)

**Why (measured 2026-09-18).** `reference/narrative.md` defines three layers: the
child's *Magic Garden*, the adult's *Chronobiological Archive* (the machine /
cosmic reading), and a hidden third — the Architect, a parent who built the
Archive to preserve the world as their child once saw it. In the code:

| Surface | Strings | Dual-voice pairs | Voice today |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `quest/config.js` (discovery labels, orb narrative, stage hints) | 26 | 11 | both, per the bible |
| `systems/discoveries.js` (idle hints, first-encounter lines) | 53 | 2 | mostly single riddles ("the gray needle", "five stolen sparks") in a vocabulary the bible does not use |
| `systems/pufflingChat.js` (context pools) | 78 | 6 | both, but the pools are keyed by situation, not by what the player has learned |
| `systems/intro/index.js` (narration cards) | 79 | 0 | terminal / archival only — the player has not chosen a perspective yet and the child layer never gets the opening |
| `world/sky.js` (wishes, constellations) | 10 | 1 | mostly single |
| `systems/wizardPufflingEvent.js` | 4 | 0 | a **third voice** ("Another dead soul", "NOW you see BOTH worlds at once!") that belongs to neither layer |
| **The Architect / parent layer** | **0** | — | **absent from every file** — the bible's emotional payoff never reaches the player |

So the owner's read is right: two label sets run in parallel, a handful of
riddles use their own words, the wizard speaks in a fourth register, and the
thing the whole design is *about* is not in the game.

| # | Item | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | **Narrative audit.** Pull every player-facing string (≈250 across the six surfaces above, plus HUD labels and the finale overlay) into one table: surface, trigger, child text, adult text, which bible term it uses, which layer it advances. Gaps and contradictions become the work list. Output: `reference/narrative-audit-2026-xx.md`. | M | none |
| K2 | **One glossary, enforced.** `narrative.md`'s terminology table becomes a module (`narrative/glossary.js`): `term('obelisk')` returns the child or adult word for the current perspective. Every surface reads from it, so "the needle", "the Big Crystal" and "Central Archive Index" stop being three names for one thing. A test asserts every string in K1's table resolves its terms through the glossary. | M | none |
| K3 | **Rewrite the single-voice surfaces.** Intro cards, sky wishes, idle hints and the wizard get child and adult versions. The wizard's lines are re-anchored: in the child layer he is a puffling who remembers the Architect; in the adult layer he is a stale maintenance process that has been running too long — both are true, which is the point. | L | none |
| K4 | **Seed the third layer as clues, not exposition** (the bible forbids dumps). Five obelisk runes already reveal per orb; give each rune a fragment — a date, a name, a child's drawing rendered as a glyph — that reads differently in each voice and only assembles after the fifth. The wizard's truth reveal becomes the moment the two readings are shown to describe the same parent. The notebook (D1) is where the fragments sit side by side. | L | none |
| K5 | **Interactions that carry story.** Three new small ones, each using existing systems: (a) standing still near a puffling house at DUSK opens a one-line memory in the current voice; (b) humming a creature's band near its glyph replays that creature's discovery line in the *other* voice; (c) after the truth reveal, Tab-switching while looking at the obelisk cross-fades its runes between child drawing and archive index. | M | low |
| K6 | **Chat that knows what you know.** `pufflingChat.js` pools gain a third key: orbs found and truth-revealed, so a puffling after the reveal does not still talk like one before it. | S | none |

*Verify:* K2's glossary test; a string-coverage test that every surface has both
voices; a scripted headless run that flips Tab at each rune and diffs the text;
owner reads the full K1 table before K3 starts — the rewrite is his voice, not
mine.

## 3. Sequencing — five sprints, each ends deployed

Ordered by player-visible value per unit of risk. Each sprint ends with tests,
build, the draw-call probe, a deploy, and an owner playtest on the 3090 and a
phone.

| Sprint | Theme | Items | What the player notices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Keep what you earned** | persistence + clarity | A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, B3, C1, C2, C6, I1, I2, J2, J5 | Progress survives a reload. The hum is taught by the first creature. The air has colour. |
| **2 — The forest is alive** | creature life + sound | E1, E2, E3, E4, F1, F2, F3, F4, C4, C5, C7 | Deer bed down at dawn, sound comes from where things are, rain lands on the pond, gusts move the whole canopy. |
| **3 — The two stories** | narrative cohesion | K1, K2, K3, K4, K5, K6 | Every line speaks in the chosen voice, the same things have the same names, and the runes start to add up to someone. |
| **4 — Reasons to stay** | notebook + free roam | D1, D2, D3, D4, I3, I4, C3, C8 | After the finale there is a book to fill — and by now it has a story to hold. |
| **5 — Runs everywhere** | performance + structure | G1, G2, G3, G4, H1–H4, J1, J3 | Steadier frame rate, phones hold the quality floor, draw calls halved. |

Sprint 1 is deliberately the least risky and the most felt. Sprint 3 sits before
the notebook on purpose: the book should be filled with reconciled text, not
text that gets rewritten after it is bound. Sprint 5 is the only one that can
threaten the FPS floor, so it goes last and is measured per step.

## 4. What this plan does not claim

- **No FPS numbers are asserted.** Every "cost" above is engineering time; the
  frame-rate effect of C3, C5, G1 and G2 is unknown until measured on hardware.
- **Audio items cannot be verified from the sandbox.** F1–F4 need ears.
- **The 200 draw-call budget is not promised.** G1+G2 is the credible path
  toward it; the current honest estimate after both is 300–450, not 200.
- **The five open TODOs are not all covered.** Puffling houses (C7) and debug
  discoverability (partly via I3 / A2) are; the jelly ritual, the receptive
  encounter flow and the 2nd-phase gate need a dedicated playtest session first
  to establish whether they are still broken after the 2026-07-01 fixes.
